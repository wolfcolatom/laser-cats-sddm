# Credits

## Made for this theme

Everything in the theme was made for it and is covered by the theme's
licence (GPL-3.0-or-later, see LICENSE): the cats (the floating laser-eyed
kittens, the roaring paw-laser planet cats and the vortex warp cat, created with
AI image tools from text descriptions, then cut out and animated for the
theme), the starfield and nebula layers, the planets (jade, Earth, amber
ringed planet, moon), rocks, smoke, flames, glow and shading, the restart and
power icons, and all the QML code.

## Classic cats (not included)

`get-cats.sh` can download these GIFs from GIPHY and make the classic cats
from them on your own computer, for your own use. They are not in this
package and not covered by its licence; they belong to their artists. The
scripts themselves (`get-cats.sh`, `get-cats/`) are part of the theme.

| Classic cat | Original | Creator |
|---|---|---|
| Floating laser cats | "Major Lazer Cat", https://giphy.com/gifs/atinum-cat-animation-ArAgo5dU2z2xO | Guldhart (@atinum on GIPHY) |
| Laser-hands planet cat | "Cat Rainbow" / cat shoots beams over a city, https://giphy.com/gifs/cat-trippy-kitty-y9hjvnO2bwJbO | unknown (GIPHY lists no creator) |
| Spiral-eyed warp cat | "Psychedelic cat dreams", https://giphy.com/gifs/cats-psychedelic-acid-l4FGoTd3NjBCmnZyE | Electric Catnip (@ElectricCatnip on GIPHY) |

If you made one of them and don't want the script to use it, open an issue
or message me and it will be taken out.
