# Where the art comes from

## Made for the theme

- **Stars, nebula, planets, rocks, glow, smoke:** drawn by the scripts in
  `tools/` (Python and ImageMagick). Most come out pixel-for-pixel the same;
  the jade and Earth planets were finished by hand afterwards.
- **Flame, shading, icons:** drawn by hand (the files are the source).
- **The cats** (the laser-eyed kittens, the roaring planet tabby and the
  vortex warp cat): made with AI image and animation tools from text
  descriptions written for the theme. No existing artwork was given to the
  tools as a reference. They were then cut out, given see-through
  backgrounds, faded at the edges and timed for the theme; the planet
  cat's lasers are drawn by the theme, following its paws frame by frame.
- **The clock font:** Space Grotesk, SIL Open Font License.

Everything above is under the theme's licence (GPL-3.0-or-later), except
the font (OFL-1.1). Purely AI-generated images may not be protected by
copyright at all in some countries; to the extent they are, they're
licensed with the theme.

## The classic cats (not included)

Before 1.4 the theme used cats cut out of GIFs on GIPHY whose artists
haven't licensed them for sharing. They are not in this repository or the
releases. `get-cats.sh` can download those GIFs and make the classic cats
on your own computer, for your own use; see [CREDITS.md](../CREDITS.md).
